The Basics

01When and where is RIDDIMWORLD?

RIDDIMWORLD is happening on Saturday 28 November 2026, from 2pm–10pm, at Riviera Beach Club, Elwood, Melbourne.

We'll be taking over the mainstage. Set times will be announced closer to the date.

02Where do I buy tickets?

All tickets are available online only through our tickets page, powered by Megatix.

Tickets will remain on sale until the event sells out. There will be no tickets available at the door.

Avoid third-party resellers. We can't guarantee tickets purchased elsewhere.

Buy Tickets

03Can I upgrade my General Admission ticket to VIP?

Yes! You can upgrade your existing General Admission ticket to VIP through the Megatix platform.

Simply create an account or log in using the email address you used to purchase your tickets. You should then be able to access your order and upgrade your tickets.

Upgrades are available until VIP tickets sell out.

04Is there an age restriction?

Yes, RIDDIMWORLD is strictly 18+.

You'll need to bring valid physical photo ID. Accepted ID includes an Australian or international driver's licence, learner permit, passport or state/territory proof of age card.

No ID, no entry.

05What time is last entry?

Last entry is 9pm. Make sure you arrive before then!

06Can I get a refund?

All ticket sales are final. Refunds won't be provided for change of mind or inability to attend.

Refunds may be available if you selected Refundable Ticket Protection at checkout, subject to XCover's terms and conditions.

If you didn't purchase this protection, refunds will only be provided where required under Australian Consumer Law or in the event of a cancellation or other circumstances where a refund is required by law.

For more information, please refer to your XCover policy.

07Is re-entry permitted?

No. RIDDIMWORLD has a strict no re-entry policy. Once you leave the festival, you won't be able to come back in, so plan accordingly.

On the Day

08What can I bring to RIDDIMWORLD?

A few things to keep in mind before heading down:

Allowed

  • Empty water bottles are allowed.
  • Sunscreen lotion is allowed.
  • Mobile phones and GoPros are allowed.

The following items are not permitted

  • Alcohol or drinks purchased outside the venue
  • Illegal drugs or prohibited substances
  • Weapons or dangerous items, including knives, laser pointers and selfie sticks
  • Glass bottles or containers
  • Large bags, suitcases or oversized backpacks
  • Umbrellas, portable seating, tables, eskies, tents or sun shelters
  • Flags, poles, signage, promotional materials or political messaging
  • Professional cameras or recording equipment without prior approval
  • Drones
  • Flares, fireworks, smoke bombs, confetti or streamers
  • Bikes, skateboards, scooters and other personal transport devices
  • Animals, excluding authorised guide dogs

All attendees will be subject to security screening. Security may also prohibit additional items where considered necessary.

09Can I bring prescription medication?

Yes, but it must be in its original packaging, with the pharmacy label clearly showing your name.

Please only bring the amount you need for the duration of the event. Medication that doesn't meet these requirements may not be permitted inside.

10Will food and drinks be available?

Yes! Food and beverage options will be available throughout the venue.

11Is there seating at RIDDIMWORLD?

RIDDIMWORLD is a standing-only festival, with no reserved or dedicated seating in the General Admission areas.

Please note that portable chairs and other seating are not permitted inside the venue.

12What should I wear?

RIDDIMWORLD is an outdoor festival, so come dressed for a day in the sun!

We recommend festival fits, breathable clothing and comfortable footwear like sneakers. You'll be on your feet dancing throughout the day.

13What if I need first aid?

First responders will be onsite throughout the festival. Any security staff member can direct you to First Aid if needed.

Getting There & Access

14How do I get there?

RIDDIMWORLD is taking place at Riviera Beach Club, 42B Marine Parade, Elwood VIC 3184.

Public Transport

We recommend catching public transport where possible.

  • Tram 16: Get off at Luna Park / The Esplanade (Stop 138), then walk approximately 15 minutes to the venue.
  • Trams 96 and 12: Travel to St Kilda, then walk approximately 15 minutes to the marina.

Plan your journey using the PTV website.

Rideshare & Drop-off

Uber and taxis are recommended. The surrounding roads can get busy, so allow extra time.

For a smoother drop-off, the venue recommends using Dickens Street and walking across at the pedestrian lights. Please avoid stopping directly on Marine Parade.

We'll share any additional event-day rideshare instructions closer to the date.

Parking

Parking around Elwood and St Kilda is very limited. There is no general public parking within the marina complex, so we recommend using public transport or rideshare.

Get directions

15Is there accessible parking and venue access?

The event grounds are wheelchair accessible, with a mix of grass and concrete areas. Please keep in mind that the grass may become soft or muddy if it rains.

Accessible parking must be arranged directly with Riviera Beach Club in advance. It cannot be organised on event day. Please contact info@rivierabeachclub.com.au to make arrangements.

A dedicated accessible toilet is located to the right of the mainstage.

Companion Card holders are entitled to an additional ticket for their carer. Please bring your Companion Card alongside your event ticket.

Please note that the VIP area is located upstairs and accessed via stairs.
